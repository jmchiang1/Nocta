Privacy Policy

What Nocta is

Nocta is a companion app for people on CPAP therapy. It reads the data your CPAP machine writes to its SD card and explains each night in plain language. Nocta is a wellness tool, not a medical device. See our Terms of Service.

In this policy, "Nocta", "we" and "us" mean the people who make Nocta. "You" means you.

What Nocta keeps about you

Nocta keeps what it needs to explain your nights:

  • Your CPAP data. The nights read from your SD card: usage hours, AHI and other event counts, leak, pressure and the machine's settings.
  • Your morning check-ins and the answers you give during setup.
  • Your equipment log, like when you last replaced your mask or filter.
  • A profile photo, if you add one.
  • Nights from a connected wearable, once they've synced (see below).

Your SD card is read in your browser; the card's files themselves are never sent anywhere. What Nocta reads from them is kept in your browser on the device you're using, and:

  • With an account, a copy is also saved to your account, so the same nights, check-ins and settings are there on every device you sign in on. Only you can read it.
  • Without an account ("Look around without an account"), everything stays in that browser and nothing is sent to our servers.

Nox, Nocta's coach, answers from replies written ahead of time, in your browser. Your data is not sent to an AI service. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy before it does.

What our servers keep

Your account. When you create an account, we keep:

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. It's stored in a scrambled (hashed) form we can't read.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture link from your Google account.
  • Whether you've finished setting up the app.
  • The saved copy of your data described above: your CPAP nights, check-ins, setup answers and photo, equipment log, and wearable nights.

If you choose "Look around without an account", nothing about you is sent to our servers.

Connected wearables. If you connect Oura, Fitbit, Whoop or Withings, that company gives Nocta an access key so Nocta can read your sleep data. Our server keeps:

  • The access key and the key used to renew it.
  • Which company it's for, when you connected, and when Nocta last synced.
  • For Withings, the user number Withings assigns you.

The access key never reaches your browser. When Nocta syncs, our server uses the key to ask the company for your recent nights and passes them to your browser. Those nights are then saved with the rest of your data, in your browser and to your account.

What Nocta reads from wearables

Nocta only asks for read access, and only to sleep data. It never writes to your wearable account.

  • Oura: sleep stages, heart rate during sleep, heart-rate variability, breathing rate and nightly blood oxygen.
  • Fitbit (through your Google account): sleep stages, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, breathing rate and nightly blood oxygen.
  • Whoop: sleep stages, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, breathing rate and blood oxygen.
  • Withings: sleep stages, heart rate and breathing rate during sleep.

Wearable data is shown next to your CPAP nights. It is not used to make medical judgments about you.

Nocta's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In plain terms: Nocta uses your Google data only to show it to you in Nocta, never for ads, and never sells it or passes it on.

Who else handles your data

Nocta uses a few service providers to run. Each only gets what it needs:

  • Supabase stores your account, the saved copy of your data, and wearable access keys, on servers in the United States.
  • Vercel hosts the website. Like most web hosts, it records basic request details such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies Nocta's typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google, which sees your IP address.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google or connect a Fitbit.
  • Oura, Whoop and Withings, only if you connect one of them.

We don't sell your data. We don't use advertising or tracking tools. We don't share your data with insurers, employers or data brokers. We would only disclose it if the law required us to.

Your choices

  • Export your data: Settings › Export my data downloads everything Nocta has about you as a file.
  • Disconnect a wearable: You › Wearables › the device › Disconnect. This deletes the access key from our server. You can also remove Nocta from that company's app or website.
  • Delete your account: Settings › Delete account. This deletes your account, the saved copy of your data and any wearable access keys from our servers, and erases Nocta's data from the device you're using. Other devices you've signed in on keep their own copy until you sign out or clear them there.
  • Erase this device's data (using Nocta without an account): Settings › Erase this device's data removes everything Nocta keeps in this browser.

Our service providers may keep backups for a short time after you delete something. Backups are not used for anything except recovering from failures.

Keeping it safe

All connections to Nocta use HTTPS. Your saved data is locked to your account: the database only lets you read or change your own. Wearable access keys are stored where only our server can read them, and they're never sent to your browser. No system is perfectly secure, but we only keep what Nocta needs to work.

Children

Nocta is for adults. It isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we keep or who sees it, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
