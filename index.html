Questions

Still deciding? Look around with sample nights.

Which machines work with Nocta?

ResMed AirSense 10 and AirSense 11. Philips machines aren’t supported yet.

Do I need an account?

Not to look around. An account saves your nights and check-ins so they show up on all your devices.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. You’ll need a computer or card reader to read the SD card. After that your nights show up on your phone too if you have an account.

Is Nocta a medical device?

No. It explains what your machine recorded. Your doctor and equipment supplier are still the people to ask about your care.
