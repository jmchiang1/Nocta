What Nocta won’t do. It’s a wellness app, not a medical device.

Nocta helps you understand your nights and talk with your doctor. It isn’t a replacement for your care team.

  • It doesn’t diagnose anything.
  • It never tells you to change your pressure or stop your therapy.
  • It doesn’t grade your numbers red, yellow or green.
  • When a pattern is worth your doctor’s look, it says so, and helps you bring the details.

Questions

Still deciding? Look around with sample nights.

Which machines work with Nocta?

ResMed AirSense 10 and AirSense 11. Philips machines aren’t supported yet.

Do I need an account?

Not to look around. You can explore a month of sample nights first. An account is how your own nights, check-ins and settings are kept, and how they show up on all your devices.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, Nocta works in your phone’s browser. Reading the SD card takes a computer with a card slot or a card reader. With an account, your nights are on your phone too after that.

Is Nocta a medical device?

No. Nocta explains what your machine recorded. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything, and it doesn’t change your therapy. Your doctor and equipment supplier are still the people to ask about your care.

Where does my data go?

The card is read in your browser and kept on your device. With an account, a copy is saved so it’s on your other devices too. The privacy policy has the details.
